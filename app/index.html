Hearth & Ledger is a working demo household budget: every figure on it is the Miller family's own ledger for November 2026, and every control — editing a budget, adding a transaction, changing a goal contribution — updates the numbers across the site.

Static site, no accounts, no network: the ledger lives in this page. Amounts in US dollars.
